How the three paths differ
  • Adventure is the original instance. Discovery and Challenge were added on top of it in patches 6.29–6.33 (2023).
  • Discovery (Polish: Poznanie) has weaker enemies, lower drop rates, less XP and psychoexperience, and lower-value loot. At most 2 players.
  • Challenge (Polish: Wyzwanie) raises every enemy stat and gives better loot. Entry costs Tracks matching your gear rank: Unrest (I–III), Dread (IV–VI), Fright (VII–IX), Terror (X–XII). Daimonite drops only on this path.
  • The game shows each instance's full loot table in its entry window; it has never been published as text, so the per-path lists here are what sources mention. The D A C marks show which paths a dungeon is confirmed to have.
